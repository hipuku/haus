Radius is named by what a thing is, not by how round it is. A control, a surface, an overlay and a
marker each have their own, so a brand can make every input rounder without touching a component,
and a product that wants square inputs and round cards can have both.

## Elevation

Three shadows, by how far from the page a thing sits: raised for a card, floating for a popover or
a menu, overlay for a modal or a drawer. A shadow is a brand entry, so a product with a flatter
look sets all three to none and every component follows.

  ]}

In dark mode a shadow does less work, because the surfaces are closer together in lightness than
they are on a white page. The dark map leans on the surface ladder for separation and keeps the
shadow for the things that really do float.
