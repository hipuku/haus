Three questions that come up whenever a palette is being built, answered by the same functions the
contrast suite runs in CI. This is [`@hipuku/colour`](/docs/packages/colour) itself, compiled to run
in the page: nothing is uploaded, and the numbers here are the numbers the tests use.

## Name a colour

The nearest of 31,900 named colours, by CIEDE2000 distance.

<NameColour />

## Compare two colours

How far apart they look, as a CIEDE2000 distance, and the WCAG contrast between them. The two
answer different questions: distance is whether a person can tell them apart, contrast is whether
one can carry text on the other.

<CompareColours />

## Analyse a palette

How evenly the colours step in lightness, how consistent their chroma is, how much of the hue wheel
they cover, which are near-duplicates, and how many pairs pass AA.

<PaletteAnalysis />
