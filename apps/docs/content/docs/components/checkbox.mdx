## Controlled or not

It is uncontrolled unless you pass `checked`. `onChange` gives you `(checked, event)`: read
`event.target.name` to tell checkboxes in one form apart.

## States

`indeterminate` shows a bar for a parent whose children are partly checked. `error` marks it
invalid and shows the message under the label; `hint` adds a line of help.

## Accessibility

- A native checkbox: Space toggles it, and it tabs as itself.
- The label is the accessible name. The hint and error are read as its description.
- `required` and `error` set `aria-required` and `aria-invalid`, and the error is not shown by
  colour alone.
