It is a Modal anchored to an edge, so it traps and returns focus, closes on Escape, keeps the page behind still, and leaves on its exit. It enters on the sheet motion role.

## Anatomy

Modal's, with `placement` set from `side`. The same backdrop, dialog, header, close button, body
and footer, and the same two class targets: `className` on the backdrop and `dialogClassName` on
the panel.

The panel is anchored to the `side` edge at full height, so `size` caps its width rather than its
measure in the middle of the page. The surface runs a little past the outer edge, so a spring that
overshoots cannot open a gap between the panel and the viewport.

## Side

`end` by default. The side is logical, so `end` is the right edge in a left-to-right page and the
left edge in a right-to-left one, and the panel enters from whichever edge that is without the
caller restating it per direction.

`placement` is omitted from the props, distributively across both halves of Modal's label union,
because `side` is the prop that sets it. A Drawer in the centre of the page is a Modal.

## Keyboard and screen reader

Modal's, unchanged. Focus moves in on open and returns on close, Tab cycles within the panel,
Escape closes it, `aria-modal` is kept by the component's own Tab and `focusin` handling, and the
page behind neither scrolls nor shifts.

A name is required, as `title` or as `aria-label`, for the reason Modal's is.

`initialFocus` and `dismissOnBackdrop` behave as they do on Modal, and the backdrop dismissal is
latched to the press at both ends in the same way.

## Motion

The sheet roles rather than the dialog roles: a slide from its edge in subtle, a spring in
expressive, and a fade in place in reduced. The travel is the panel's own width, so it arrives
from off-screen; in reduced the sheet offset is zero and the travel collapses to nothing, which is
what makes it a fade.

The backdrop and the panel's parts follow Modal's sequence, and the panel stays in the document
until its exit has run.

## Decisions

- **It is a Modal, not a second dialog implementation.** A drawer is a dialog in every way that
  matters: focus is trapped and returned, Escape closes it, and the page behind holds still.
  Writing it again would mean keeping two copies of that in step.
- **Promoted because the roles existed and nothing used them.** vault draws a drawer, and haus had
  the sheet motion roles with no component reaching for them.
- **`side` rather than `placement`.** The prop name says what the component is for, and the union
  it narrows to is the two edges a drawer can come from.
