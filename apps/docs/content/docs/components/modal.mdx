A dialog that traps focus and gives it back. The types require either a `title` or an `aria-label`, because a dialog with no accessible name is announced as nothing.

`initialFocus` points focus at the action rather than the container, and `dismissOnBackdrop` can refuse a backdrop click, worth turning off for anything destructive or anything holding unsaved input.
